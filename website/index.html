The Age of Dinosaurs

252 – 66 million years ago (and, apparently, tonight). Under three skylights, the King stands on his steel mount, which stays put when the T-Rex… doesn't.

THE PLATFORM IS EMPTY.

Tyrannosaurus rex

“The Tyrant Lizard King.” 12 m long, 8 tonnes, teeth like bananas. Fun fact: despite its size, T. rex had only TWO fingers on each tiny hand.

Triceratops

Three horns, one frill, zero chill. Ate plants. Probably fine.

Velociraptor

Turkey-sized. Feathery. Smaller than the movies told you. Still bites.

Real meteorite

It fell out of the sky. It is older than the dinosaurs. Please do not lick the meteorite.

Ammonites

Squid in spiral shells. They're on the front of our building, too. Basically sea cinnamon rolls.

Dig site

Real sand! Fake bones. Kids only. (Guards: no digging on the clock.)

Egyptian Wing New wing · Mummy Mania

The Land of the Pharaohs

Sandstone walls carved with hieroglyphs, a midnight-blue ceiling full of gold stars, three Anubis statues (one of them knocked over, for reasons), a sphinx, an obelisk, and a sarcophagus that is absolutely, definitely closed.

…donut?

The mummy

Wrapped in about 20 layers of linen, 3,000 years ago. Favorite food: unknown. (Probably not donuts.)

Tip: stare at the sarcophagus for a few seconds.

The ankh

The ancient symbol of life. It is not a key. We checked.

Scarab beetle

Scarabs roll balls of poop across the desert. The Egyptians thought that was beautiful. They were right.

Egyptian numbers

∩ means ten. | means one. So ∩∩||| is twenty-three. You may need this later. (Tonight.)

The four pillars

Canopic jars · tap one

Four jars for the four guardians: the jackal, the falcon, the baboon and the human. They hold… things. Please don't open the jars. (Fine. One jar.)

Roman Empire S · P · Q · R

All roads lead to the museum

A Pompeii-style hall: red painted panels, black bands, a gold meander running round the top, a coffered ceiling with gilded rosettes, and eight white columns. Plus five emperors, and Caligula, who has to stand in the corner.

Chariot

Top speed: 40 km/h. Seatbelts: none. Cupholders: also none.

Gladiator shield

Property of Maximus. Do not borrow. Won XXIII fights. Lost I (to a goose).

Roman numerals 101

I = 1 · V = 5 · X = 10 · L = 50 · C = 100 · D = 500 · M = 1000. A small one before a big one means subtract: IV = 4, XL = 40.

Questions we get a lot
